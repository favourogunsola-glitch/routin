The Best Fitness routine!

This shows you the best routine to use if you want to be in prim and proper shape!

Morning routine

Good Morning Stretch: Hinge back with a long spine and gently open your chest to mobilize the back and hips.

Time: 1 minute

Cardio routine

March in Place, Jumping Jacks, High Knees, Butt Kicks, Shadow Boxing, Skaters, and Slow Deep Breaths.

Time: 20 minutes

Building strenght

Push-ups, Bodyweight Squats, Glute Bridges, Plank, and Inverted Rows or Superman extensions

Time: 15 minutes

Yoga routine

A gentle morning yoga routine combines Child's Pose, Cat-Cow, Downward-Facing Dog, Cobra Pose, and Mountain Pose to stretch stiff muscles and improve mobility.

Time: 15 minutes

Simple evening routine

Go for a walk to relax your mind and body after a tough day of work and other activities

Time: 25 minutes

Make sure to

Drink enough water and take proper rest after completing your workout.

Time: All day

Best fitness advice:Consistency always beats intensity when it comes to long-term fitness results. Working out for just 10 to 15 minutes every single day builds lasting habits and physical strength much faster than doing one grueling, two-hour workout once a week.
